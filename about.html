About.

AI, automation and teaching professional helping small businesses and individuals work smarter with technology.

01 — The story

I'm Santhosh Kumar, an AI, automation and teaching professional. I help small businesses and individuals work smarter with technology.

Based in Toronto, I've spent over a decade in enterprise technology, working across SAP and Oracle on global ERP migrations and automation that turned hours of manual work into minutes. Now I take those same skills and make them accessible to small businesses and everyday operators who deserve the same advantages as large corporations.

I do two things. I teach people how to use AI and automation in plain language, and I build the agents and systems that do the heavy lifting for them.

"Most small businesses don't have a technology problem. They have a clarity problem. They don't know what to automate first."

Whether it's your first AI tool, your first automated workflow, or a full agent stack, I meet you where you are and move you forward.
